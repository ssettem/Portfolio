Competitive Targeting at EdzeQ

INSIDE SALES ASSOCIATE · COMPETITIVE ANALYSIS
EdzeQ Technologies · Dec 2018 – Mar 2020

EdzeQ was my first job out of undergrad, and the fastest way to learn that untargeted outreach is just noise. Analyzing competitor course portfolios across 8 regional markets told me how to reposition our consumer sales presentations, and that discipline is what turned into $48K+ in revenue.

What I actually built

01

Reading the competitive landscape before making a single call

I analyzed competitor course portfolios across 8 regional markets first, instead of working a generic call list the same way in every territory.

02

Turning that read into a target list, not a spray list

The regional analysis fed strategic, data-driven targeting, so outreach went to the accounts the data said were actually worth the time.

03

Conversion moved because the targeting did

That shift lifted the call-to-meeting conversion rate to 22%, and the incremental revenue followed from there.
