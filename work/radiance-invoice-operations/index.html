Invoice Operations at Radiance

OPERATIONS ANALYST · INVOICING · SLA TRACKING
Radiance · Nov 2023 – Aug 2024

Every day, 150+ merchant invoicing requests and purchase orders came in for an e-commerce client’s merchant platform. My job was to audit each one against the campaign terms it was raised under, inside a 24-hour SLA, and to fix the process problems that kept sending work back.

Where the SLA was actually breaking

Merchant onboarding was running late, and an average turnaround number hid why. I wrote SQL queries to track P90 turnaround time by merchant category, the time within which 90% of approvals finish, so the slow tail showed up instead of being averaged away. It pointed at one category.

What the P90 query showed for large accounts

Merchant onboarding approvals completed within 7 days.

Measured (P90)
Large accounts sat in a 14-day approval bottleneck.
SLA BREACH: large accounts took twice the agreed time
The root cause of the onboarding delays was one merchant category, not the whole queue.

What I actually did

01

Audited the daily queue against campaign terms

I processed 150+ merchant invoicing requests and purchase orders a day, checking each against the campaign terms it was raised under, within a 24-hour SLA turnaround target.

02

Took a 35% invoice rejection rate to under 1%

Excel Pivot Tables across 5,000+ monthly records isolated a 35% invoice rejection rate. I lowered rejections for those specific corporate accounts to under 1% over a 6-month period through VLOOKUP matching and custom Salesforce validation parameters I designed with developers.

03

Wrote the requirements engineering needed to fix a crash

The platform froze during large merchant data uploads. I partnered with the software engineering teams and authored a technical requirements document detailing the file size limits that triggered the crashes.

04

Made the process teachable

I acted as the team’s subject matter expert on client promotional rules, mentoring junior analysts through standardized processing guides.

05

Kept account managers and merchants unblocked

I supported internal account managers and external merchants in monitoring queue velocity KPIs and resolving critical SLA billing breaches.
