Team Analytics at PropSquare

DASHBOARDS · FORECASTING
PropSquare · Mar 2022 – Nov 2023

Growing a team of 8 representatives isn’t just coaching, it’s giving them numbers to coach against. I built daily Tableau performance dashboards the team actually checked every morning, backed by SQL and Excel forecasting models that gave leadership weekly revenue visibility instead of a monthly surprise, driving 31%+ team output growth.

From gut feel to a number everyone could see

Before the dashboards, performance visibility meant whatever a rep remembered to mention in a standup. Putting the same daily numbers in front of the whole team, not just leadership, is what let reps course-correct mid-week instead of finding out at month-end.
