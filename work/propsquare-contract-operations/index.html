Corporate facility service contracts were stalling, because every pricing quote was drafted by hand. I built automated Excel calculators that generated the quotes instead, which unblocked $300K+ in stalled contract billing, and cut contract turnaround from 18 days to under 48 hours.

From hand-drafted quotes to a repeatable workflow

Manual drafting was slow and it introduced pricing typos, and each contract waited on vendor onboarding validation steps nobody had written down. Fixing both is what moved the turnaround.

What I actually built

01

Pricing calculators instead of hand-drafted quotes

I built automated Excel calculators that generated contract pricing quotes systematically, replacing manual drafting steps and eliminating pricing typos. That unblocked $300K+ in stalled corporate facility service contract billing.

02

One documented onboarding workflow

I standardized and documented the vendor onboarding validation steps into a repeatable workflow for the sales team, cutting contract completion turnaround from 18 days to under 48 hours.

03

Daily check-ins on where deals were stalling

As the go-to process expert, I mentored a team of 20 sales representatives, running daily data-review check-ins and using interactive Tableau KPI dashboards to identify exactly where specific deals were stalling in the pipeline.
